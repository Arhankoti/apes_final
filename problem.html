Problem

What is the problem?

The problem is the spread and large outbreaks of mountain pine beetles in pine forests. Mountain pine beetles are small bark beetles that attack and kill pine trees by drilling under the bark and stopping the movement of water and nutrients through the tree.

How does it connect to HIPPCO?

This issue fits HIPPCO under invasive species, climate change, and habitat destruction. Even though pine beetles are originally native to western North America, rising temperatures have helped them spread into new places and higher elevations where forests were not used to major beetle outbreaks.

Why should people care?

This is an important problem because beetle outbreaks kill millions of trees, increase wildfire danger, lower carbon storage, hurt the timber industry, and damage forest ecosystems.
