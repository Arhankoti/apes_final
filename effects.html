Human and Ecosystem Effects

Human Effects

  • Wildfire risk
  • Timber loss
  • Cost of tree removal
  • Smoke and air quality

The pine beetle outbreaks affect humans because dead trees increase wildfire risk, damage forest, and make areas more dangerous from falling trees. The timber industry can lose money because valuable trees die and the government must spend money removing dead trees and managing wildfires. Human health can be also affected because wildfire smoke can cause trouble breathing and poor air quality.

Ecosystem Effects

  • Tree death
  • Habitat loss
  • Carbon cycle impact
  • Whitebark pine and lodgepole pine

The pine beetles harm ecosystems by killing large numbers of pine trees and damaging forest habitats. The areas most at risk are forests in western North America. Whitebark pine is a K selected specialist species and has experienced major tree loss in the Greater Yellowstone Ecosystem. Lodgepole pine is also heavily affected in many Colorado forests. Dead trees increase wildfire risk and affect the carbon cycle because they stop absorbing carbon dioxide.
